Summary of 2007 wt20:

The 2007 ICC World Twenty20 was the inaugural Men's T20 World Cup, hosted by South Africa from September 11 to 24, 2007. Featuring 12 teams, the tournament famously catapulted the sport's shortest format into the global mainstream and introduced high-octane entertainment that eventually paved the way for the Indian Premier League.
Led by a young and relatively untested MS Dhoni, India entered with low expectations but rallied to a historic victory.The tournament featured iconic moments like Yuvraj Singh's six sixes in an over against England and a thrilling bowl-out win over Pakistan in the group stage. The two arch-rivals met again in the nail-biting Johannesburg final, where India successfully defended 157 runs. In the dramatic final over bowled by Joginder Sharma, Misbah-ul-Haq attempted a scoop shot and was caught by Sreesanth, securing a historic 5-run victory for India.
